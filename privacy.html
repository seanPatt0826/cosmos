Privacy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Nothing you type leaves your browser

Cosmos has no accounts, no sign-in, no database and no server that processes anything you do. The entire simulation runs inside the page on your own device. If you enter a list of names, those names are never transmitted anywhere, never logged, and never visible to anyone but you.

What is stored on your device

Cosmos saves two things in your browser's local storage so it remembers your setup between visits:

  • The list of names you entered
  • Whether you chose light or dark mode

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not used to identify or track you. Clearing your browser's site data removes it permanently, and the site works normally without it. Cosmos sets no cookies of its own; advertising, described below, may set cookies.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs when a page is requested. These typically include your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, and are used for security and to keep the service running. See Vercel's own privacy policy for details of their handling.

Fonts and libraries

The page loads its typeface from Google Fonts and its physics library from Cloudflare's CDN. Requesting those files reveals your IP address to those providers, as loading any third-party resource on the web does. Neither is used to identify you here.

Advertising

This site may display advertising supplied by third-party partners, including Google. Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Where required by law, personalised advertising is shown only after you have given consent, and you can change or withdraw that consent at any time.

Children

Cosmos is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from them.

Your rights

Because the site collects no personal information beyond the hosting logs described above, there is generally nothing held about you to access, correct or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address published on the site owner's contact page.
